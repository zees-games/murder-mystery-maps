# Import notes

The importer command and choices used for each converted map. Maintainer notes; the README has the credits table. The flags are also in `tools/ports.txt`.

`mm_mountainresort` was converted with `import_bsp.py gm_mountainresort.bsp mm_mountainresort "Mountain Resort" prvec https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2312309380 --terrain --floor=1.2 --min-size=0.5 --cover=30 --spread-spawns=14 --no-doors`. The map has no doors. Workshop terms on the page: "Do not reupload any content. Also it cannot be used in paid work you do without my consent"; no licence permitting redistribution is stated. **Flagged: the owner decided to convert it anyway; the arrangement is credit, link and removal on request, and the map is removed on request by the author.** Only the layout is used (boxes, doors, starts); every texture, model and sound is a free replacement or absent.


Converted with `tools/import_bsp.py` (reads through dot-map-convert, cloned beside this repository or at `$DOTBSP_PATH`): `import_bsp.py mu_silence.bsp mm_silence_hollow "Hollow Hill" "Danny Judas" <url> --dusk --widen-doors --cover=18 --skip-door=9.58,0.0 --skip-door=24.54,-5.51 --ambience=amb_outdoor,amb_indoor`. The file is written once and kept; `build_maps.py` lists it in `IMPORTED` and `--check` only requires that it exists.

Office was converted with `import_bsp.py mu_office.bsp mm_office_murder "Office" Kammorne <url> --dusk --widen-doors --hang-doorways --dress=24 --ambience=amb_indoor,amb_indoor` (the Workshop archive is unpacked with the plain GMAD reader first). The source has no door entities for its meeting rooms and offices (only the lift doors), so `--hang-doorways` hangs a door in every 1.4 m doorway under a header (28 doors) and removes the author's own leaf standing in it, then settles the spawns again; `--dress=24` adds seeded plants, a bookcase, a sofa, bins and coat racks on open floor; the carpet and the painted walls take their colours by texture name in `TEXTURE_MAP`.

Greenwood Estate was converted with `import_bsp.py mu_greenwood.bsp mm_greenwood_estate "Greenwood Estate" Kammorne <url> --dusk --widen-doors --hang-doorways --dress=60 --dress-walls=80 --spread-spawns=24 --ambience=amb_outdoor,amb_indoor` (the Workshop archive unpacked with the plain GMAD reader). The author's own door entities give 7 doors; `--dress=60` adds seeded furniture on open floor; `--dress-walls=80` sets beds, shelves, cabinets, sofas, lamps and washers with their backs to the walls of every roofed room, chosen by floor texture and storey (tile is a kitchen or utility room, concrete a store, wood a lounge below and a bedroom above), keeping 0.7 m clear in front, stairs' feet and a 1.9 m square round every door free; `--spread-spawns=24` drops the author's starts that stand within 3.5 m of another (they come in pairs 1.2 m apart) and tops up with the clearest open floor, farthest first, a share to each floor (cellar, ground, upper, yard).

Hotel was converted with `import_bsp.py mu_hotel.bsp mm_mu_hotel "Hotel" Neo_Wesker https://gamebanana.com/maps/206520 --dusk --widen-doors --hang-doorways --dress=60 --dress-walls=80 --spread-spawns=24 --ambience=amb_wind,amb_indoor` (the GameBanana archive unpacked with `unrar`): the same options as Greenwood Estate, 24 spawns over its floors, and every texture, model and sound replaced by this game's own CC0 art.

Villa was converted with `DOTBSP_PATH=~/godot/dot-map-convert tools/import_bsp.py gm_scarface.bsp mm_villa "Villa" korka007 https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2633639653 --dusk --widen-doors --hang-doorways --dress=60 --dress-walls=80 --spread-spawns=24 --max-lights=64 --prune-pockets --terrain --ambience=amb_outdoor,amb_indoor --merge-boxes --min-box-volume=0.05 --min-box-thickness=0.15 --max-boxes=5500 --terrain-budget=6000 --max-wire=55000 --skip-door=-11.601,18.345 --skip-door=-8.02,18.345` (the Workshop archive unpacked with the plain GMAD reader; the flags are also in `tools/ports.txt`, and the importer reproduces the committed document with no hand edit): the source has one start, so 24 spawns are spread over its floors; the author's ground is a displacement heightfield; 64 of 269 lights kept. An unbudgeted import is 17,414 boxes, far larger than the budgets used here; the game has no cap on a map (its `max_map_*` limits default to 0, any value, 0 = unlimited, and a map past 60,000 bytes is sent in pieces), so the budgets below are this pack's choice for bots, load time and browsers, not a game limit. The merge and trim passes leave 9,840, `--max-boxes=5500` cuts the smallest unprotected boxes (the stair treads and walls the bots need are kept), and `--max-wire=55000` (dot-map-convert's deflated-size budget, which also rounds to centimetres) cuts further until the document deflates to 55,000 bytes by its own measure, a size the game would also take in one message: 4,021 boxes are left. The terrain heightfield is 2 m (`--terrain-budget=6000`) to leave room. The two doors `--hang-doorways` would hang at (-11.601, 18.345) and (-8.02, 18.345) swing into walls, so `--skip-door` leaves them open; 13 doors are hung.
Murder Mansion was converted with `import_bsp.py test_map2.bsp mm_murder_mansion "Murder Mansion" Grimm <url> --dusk --widen-doors --hang-doorways --dress=60 --dress-walls=80 --spread-spawns=24 --ambience=amb_outdoor,amb_indoor --merge-boxes --min-box-volume=0.05` (the Workshop archive unpacked with the plain GMAD reader). The source has no door entities and `--hang-doorways` found no doorway to hang (0 doors), so the rooms are open.
Deco Mansion was converted with `import_bsp.py gm_deco_mansion.bsp mm_deco_mansion "Deco Mansion" Gastly_Furby_777 https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3698815153 --dusk --widen-doors --max-lights=128 --prune-pockets --spread-spawns=24 --skip-start=2 --ambience=amb_outdoor,amb_indoor --merge-boxes --min-box-volume=0.05 --max-boxes=5500 --max-wire=55000 --one-way=0.895,-48.39 --skip-door=54.826,-38.198 --blurb=...` (the Workshop archive unpacked with the plain GMAD reader; the flags are also in `tools/ports.txt`, and the importer reproduces the committed document with no hand edit). An unbudgeted import is 14,126 boxes, a large count for bots and browsers (the game sets no limit). The merge and trim passes leave 6,566, `--max-boxes=5500` cuts the smallest unprotected boxes and `--max-wire=55000` (dot-map-convert's deflated-size budget, which also rounds to centimetres) cuts further until the document deflates to 55,000 bytes by its own measure, a size that still fits one message (the game sends larger maps in pieces): 4,224 boxes are left, 51 doors hung (the one at 0.895, -48.39 is pinned one-way because it swings into a wall) and 452 props kept (the importer drops 40 left hanging or buried by the cuts). Every texture and model is replaced by this game's own CC0 art and the author's two music files are not carried. The author's 140 doors give 51 hung here; 21 more found no doorway beside the hinge and are left open. The four freight lifts (moving brush entities) have no equivalent in the format and are left out, so the three storeys are joined by the mansion's stairs only. The 3D-skybox surroundings are dropped by the importer's out-of-reach pass.
Functionalist Night was converted with `import_bsp.py <bsp> mm_functionalist_night "Functionalist Night" VITAPLEX https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3382694345 --dusk --widen-doors --hang-doorways --dress=60 --dress-walls=80 --spread-spawns=24 --max-lights=100 --prune-pockets --terrain --ambience=amb_wind,amb_indoor --merge-boxes --min-box-volume=0.02 --one-way=12.344,-79.019` (the Workshop archive unpacked with the plain GMAD reader; the flags are also in `tools/ports.txt`): the box budget joins touching boxes and drops trim under 0.02 m3 (an unbudgeted import is near 9000 boxes, a large count for bots and browsers (the game sets no limit); a stricter 0.05 m3 floor also drops the door jambs and loses most hung doors), 12 doors hung (the door at -10.364, -5.944 made by `--hang-doorways` is pinned `away: false` in the JSON by hand, because `--one-way` runs before it), 23 spawns spread (the 24th stood over a hole in the heightfield and was removed by hand) over its floors, the ground a heightfield, and no model, texture or sound of the author's or of the packed commercial content is carried.

Rooftops 2016 was converted with `tools/ports.txt`'s line for it (`--max-boxes=5500 --max-wire=55000` keep it inside the game's catalogue limits; `--skip-door` and `--drop-beside` clear one door and two props the audit flagged). The ids `mm_villa`, `mm_functionalist_night` and `mm_sunken_resort` were `gm_villa`, `functionalist_night` and `sunken_resort`, renamed because the hide-n-seek pack uses the same ids and the catalogue refuses a second map with an id.

Sunken Resort was converted with `DOTBSP_PATH=~/godot/dot-map-convert tools/import_bsp.py gm_apresort.bsp mm_sunken_resort "Sunken Resort" Praisemantas https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3376822715 --dusk --widen-doors --max-lights=128 --prune-pockets --terrain --spread-spawns=24 --ambience=amb_indoor,amb_indoor --max-boxes=5500` (the Workshop archive unpacked with the plain GMAD reader). The source has 6 starts; 24 spawns are spread over 6 floors. 27 doors were hung and 12 of the author's doors found no doorway, so those rooms stay open; most of the props (chairs, keyboards, screens) have no stand-in in this catalogue and were dropped. Every texture, model and sound is this game's own CC0 art; none of the author's files is carried. The importer's hard box budget `--max-boxes=5500` is this pack's own budget (the game has no limit: default 6000 is gone, any value, 0 = unlimited): 10942 boxes become 5500, the smallest cut first, keeping floors, stairs, door frames and the ground under spawns, props and lights (17 of 24 spawns reachable from the first, before and after the cut). The kept document was then post-processed with `tools/trim_wire.py maps/mm_sunken_resort.json --min-volume=0.08` (5500 boxes become 4680, rounded to centimetres; about 52 KB on the wire against the 60 KB single-message size (a larger map is sent in pieces)) and `tools/add_cover.py maps/mm_sunken_resort.json --count=18` (18 sofas, cabinets, beds, desks and shelves on clear floor beside the spawns, for something to hide behind).
Factory Winter was converted the same way, with the snowed ground and the doors that the map hangs: `import_bsp.py <bsp> mm_factory_winter "Factory Winter" FoKa <url> --dusk --max-lights=128 --skip-start=22 --terrain=snow --drop-beside=15.18,0.15,furniture_television_vintage --drop-beside=-3.34,0.31,furniture_books --drop-beside=-34.25,6.42,furniture_lounge_sofa` plus `--one-way=x,z` for the six doors that swing one way (-35.721,7.048; 5.179,-8.153; -38.148,3.315; -27.411,7.048; -31.366,3.315; -46.72,15.735) and `--skip-door=x,z` for the five that stay open (-3.658,3.796; -29.718,17.288; 14.44,2.315; -43.091,14.602; -46.835,21.793), and `--ambience=amb_wind,amb_industrial`. The importer cuts every brush with dot-map's `boxes.decompose`, so wedges are wedges.

The five converted maps take their air from the author's file (`import_bsp.py`'s `environment()`): the sun's colour, brightness and angle from `light_environment`, the shade's colour and strength from its ambient, the fog's colour, start, end and density cap from `env_fog_controller` (in metres), the sky from the sky's name (a mood painted in code, never the author's sky textures: `office` is an overcast afternoon, `sky_day02_09` an overcast winter one, `jungle` a clear day, `militia_hdr` a clear dusk), snow or rain volumes from `func_precipitation` (Factory Winter only, two volumes over the yard, so nothing falls indoors), and a lamp for each `point_spotlight` and `env_fire`. Their sound is dot-audio's CC0 stock chosen by what the author's soundscapes name (`--ambience=<outdoor>,<indoor>`): wind outdoors for the hotel and the factory, a breeze for Hollow Hill and Greenwood, room tone indoors, machinery under the factory's roofs; no sound from any of the author's files is carried. A finished map can take a rerun of just this with `--env-only=maps/<id>.json` (Hollow Hill, whose geometry is hand-edited): `import_bsp.py mu_silence.bsp --env-only=maps/mm_silence_hollow.json --ambience=amb_outdoor,amb_indoor --fog=14,40,0.55` (the author's fog, 11 to 18 m, buried the town from the overview; this one keeps the haze and loses the wall). Hollow Hill's flooded cellar is a see-through `water` box set into its basement by hand (the author's file has no water), drawn with a CC0 sea-foam photograph tinted blue-green. A hotel skin that is drawn in a floor material under a roof is retextured as ceiling by `retexture_ceilings`.

Map limits are settings in the game, not constants: `max_map_boxes`, `max_map_props`, `max_map_lights`, `max_map_spawns`, `max_map_doors` and `max_map_toys` (default 0, any value, 0 = unlimited), and `map_lights_warn` (default 128, a warning past that many lights, never a cut). The importer's `--max-lights=N` follows: default 0 keeps every light; a set N thins them and prints a warning with the count dropped.
